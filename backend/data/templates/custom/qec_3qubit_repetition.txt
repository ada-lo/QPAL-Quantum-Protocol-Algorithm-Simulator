NOTE QEC: 3-Qubit Bit-Flip Repetition Code

INIT q0 +
INIT q1
INIT q2

LABEL Encode logical qubit into 3 physical qubits
CNOT q0 q1
CNOT q0 q2

LABEL Simulate bit-flip error on q1
NOTE (Comment out to test no-error case)
X q1

LABEL Syndrome measurement ancillas
INIT q3
INIT q4

LABEL Parity check: q0 XOR q1
CNOT q0 q3
CNOT q1 q3

LABEL Parity check: q1 XOR q2
CNOT q1 q4
CNOT q2 q4

LABEL Measure syndromes
MEASURE q3 BASIS Z
MEASURE q4 BASIS Z

NOTE Syndrome 10 → flip q0, 11 → flip q1, 01 → flip q2
NOTE Apply correction then decode
CNOT q0 q1
CNOT q0 q2

LABEL Readout logical qubit
MEASURE q0 BASIS Z
