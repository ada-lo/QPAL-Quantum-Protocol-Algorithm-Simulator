NOTE N-Qubit Teleportation (2-qubit payload example)

ACTOR Alice
ACTOR Bob

LABEL Payload qubits to teleport
INIT q0 +
INIT q1 +

LABEL Bell channel 1 for q0
INIT q2
INIT q3
H q2
CNOT q2 q3
ASSIGN q2 Alice
ASSIGN q3 Bob

LABEL Bell channel 2 for q1
INIT q4
INIT q5
H q4
CNOT q4 q5
ASSIGN q4 Alice
ASSIGN q5 Bob

LABEL Teleport q0
CNOT q0 q2
H q0
MEASURE q0 BASIS Z
MEASURE q2 BASIS Z
SEND q2 Alice Bob

LABEL Teleport q1
CNOT q1 q4
H q1
MEASURE q1 BASIS Z
MEASURE q4 BASIS Z
SEND q4 Alice Bob
